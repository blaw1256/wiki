City of Scholars

General Information

The City of Scholars is arguably one of the most bizarre places in Ariaunus. This nations boasts an impressive total of 1 City in its borders, as the City of Scholars takes up the entirety of the island it rests upon. Great walls surround the coasts, largely for show but the point is obvious. Entering the City of Scholars reveals a web of interconnected cityscape that is largely impossible to navigate for the average person. To add to the marvel, the City of Scholars is the only nation to not use the conventional money system. Instead the City of Scholars deals in Information.

Climate: Temperate
Population: 7 million
Government: Diplomatic Academia
Ruler: Lumeretes Fudrullin
Capital City: The City of Scholars

Information

Information is the blood that pumps the beating heart that is the City of Scholars. The City of Scholars has long done away with the conventional gold system and instead buys and sells information. The actual information being passed around is largely unnimportant, what matters is the size of the information. A drink in a tavern may only cost you a fun fact you heard 27 years ago, a night of stay is something fun you did in the last year while a citadel may be worth a prized trade secret. The intricacies of what you are say are irrelevant, only that you have it to say. Due to regulations, you cannot give the same information to the person who gave it to you. This was instated in 1156 PA after the famous Goblin Anatomy sting revealed a group of at least 500 people all paying for goods and services with the same piece of information.

The other relevant part of dealing in information is whether the trade is Permanent or not. Most trades done with information are Not Permanent, that is to say that once the transaction is complete, both sides remember the information. However, if one party wants the trade be more serious, they can request that the trade be Permanent, and that the person giving the information will not remember it after the trade has completed. This is a rare occurence however, and is usually only used by high up political negotiations. Rumors have long been present that suggest there are underground markets that deal in Permanent trades in order to blackmail their members.
