City of Scholars

General Information

The City of Scholars is arguably one of the most bizarre places in Ariaunus. This nations boasts an impressive total of 1 City in its borders, as the City of Scholars takes up the entirety of the island it rests upon. Great walls surround the coasts, largely for show but the point is obvious. Entering the City of Scholars reveals a web of interconnected cityscape that is largely impossible to navigate for the average person. To add to the marvel, the City of Scholars is the only nation to not use the conventional money system. Instead the City of Scholars deals in Information.

Climate: Temperate
Population: 7 million
Government: Diplomatic Academia
Ruler: Lumeretes Fudrullin
Capital City: The City of Scholars
